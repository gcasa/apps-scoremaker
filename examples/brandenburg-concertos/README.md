# Bach Brandenburg Concertos, BWV 1046–1051 — ScoreMaker examples

These eighteen `.score` files contain all six Brandenburg Concertos, separated into
independently openable movements. The two closing movements of Concerto No. 3 share one file:
its Adagio consists of only two sustained chords leading into the Allegro finale.

The files retain complete multi-track note data, movement tempo and meter, MIDI velocity, and
instrument programs. They are intended as complete playback and notation examples rather than
critical engraved editions.

## Contents

- Concerto No. 1 in F major, BWV 1046: `01-01` through `01-04`
- Concerto No. 2 in F major, BWV 1047: `02-01` through `02-03`
- Concerto No. 3 in G major, BWV 1048: `03-01` and combined `03-02`
- Concerto No. 4 in G major, BWV 1049: `04-01` through `04-03`
- Concerto No. 5 in D major, BWV 1050: `05-01` through `05-03`
- Concerto No. 6 in B-flat major, BWV 1051: `06-01` through `06-03`

The composition is public domain. Concertos 1–5 were converted from the anonymous
no-copyright MIDI transcriptions distributed by Kunst der Fuge:
https://kunstderfuge.com/bach/variae.htm

Concerto No. 6 was converted from Christian Mondrup's recorder-and-continuo edition hosted by
IMSLP/WIMA under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0. The arrangement preserves all
three movements but uses recorders in place of the original viola and viola-da-gamba scoring:
https://imslp.org/wiki/Brandenburg_Concerto_No.6%2C_BWV_1051_%28Bach%2C_Johann_Sebastian%29
